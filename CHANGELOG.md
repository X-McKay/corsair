With N above 1 only baseline languages with at least N cases decide `language_regressed`, `accuracy_not_improved` and `accuracy_below_baseline` (macro accuracy, its minimum gain and its band read over those languages); the others are shown as `reported only (single case)` or `reported only (<n> cases)`. The gate then reads both runs' trajectories (beside the scorecard, else `<gym root>/runs/<run id>/`, only when they reproduce the scorecard) into `GateVerdict.paired` (new `PairedOutcomes`, `PairedCounts` and `PairedCase` contracts): per case present on both sides, its majority outcome baseline versus candidate counted as `pass_to_fail`, `fail_to_pass`, `stable_pass` and `stable_fail` (overall and per language), the flipped case ids per direction, cases only one side scored, and the deciding flips outside the noise band (a flip in a language whose band is ±1.0 is within noise). New reason `paired_regression` rejects an otherwise accepted accuracy or objective gate whose deciding cases fell more than they rose; `render_gate` prints the paired table. Without `--min-cases` every decision, view and ledger cell is unchanged and earlier verdicts decode with `min_cases` 1. `objective_terms` and `case_outcomes` in `evaluation/gym/scoring.py` are the one definition of J and of a case's majority outcome. See the [evaluation-gym guide](docs/guides/evaluation-gym.md#deciding-languages-and-paired-outcomes).
- Signal slices (s18): `gate(..., slice=True)` and `corsair gym gate --slice [--root DIR]` gate a sliced candidate (`gym run tier1 --repeats 5 --case …`) against an unsliced or identically sliced baseline on the candidate's cases only, by their paired per-case majority outcomes from both runs' trajectories (refused, exit 2, when either is missing or does not reproduce its scorecard, and with `--smoke`, a non-objective `--track`, `--target-language` or `--min-cases`). New reasons `slice_regressed` (a slice case went pass to fail outside the noise band) and `slice_not_improved` (none went fail to pass outside it) reject; `slice_mismatch` (a case the baseline did not score, or a differently sliced baseline), different datasets or budgets, fewer than three repeats over the slice and a band of another dataset, budgets or baseline repeat count are INCOMPARABLE. With a band, a flip in a language whose band is a whole case counts neither way. `GateVerdict` adds `slice`, `slice_cases` and `slice_repeats`; J, macro accuracy, the token and seconds ratios and the language deltas are recomputed over the slice (`slice_scorecard`) and never decide; the view's header reads `Gate (slice)` and says the result is a slice screen, not an acceptance. `LedgerRow` adds an optional `slice` `TierGate` (`gym ledger append --slice RUN-DIR [--reference-tier1 REF] [--root DIR]`), rendered in the Waves table's tier1 cell as `slice: <gate> (<n> cases × <repeats>)`; a row whose only gate is a slice cannot be accepted or merged. Without `--slice` a sliced scorecard stays INCOMPARABLE as before, and existing verdicts and ledger rows decode and render unchanged. The loop runs a slice screen (target cases plus two same-language stable-pass sentinels, five repeats) before the full tier1 run with `--min-cases 3 --noise …`. See the [evaluation-gym guide](docs/guides/evaluation-gym.md#signal-slices).
- Reference selection in the identity (s19): `runtime_skill_digest` hashes the reference selection tables (`_REFERENCE_CLASS_SELECTORS`, `_SELECTABLE_RECIPE_KINDS`, `_RECIPE_KIND_REFERENCES`) as canonical JSON ahead of the admitted resource bytes, so a change to which kinds or classes receive a packaged note changes both roles' skills digests and the recorded run identity; before, a table-only change recorded the same identity as its reference (found by h5). The selection itself is unchanged. Every run after this records new `producer_skills` and `auditor_skills` digests once.
- Per-case stability in the noise band (s20): `corsair gym noise` reads each run's trajectories (beside its scorecard, else `<gym root>/runs/<run id>/`) and `NoiseBand` adds `cases` (`NoiseCase`: per run the case's majority outcome and verified episode count, and a `stability` of `stable_pass`, `stable_fail` or `unstable`), with `cases_unknown` (`trajectories_missing`) and `cases_unknown_runs` when a run's trajectories are missing or do not reproduce its scorecard; the schema stays `corsair.gym-noise/1` and existing bands decode with `cases` null. With a band, a paired flip of an `unstable` case is within noise in the full gate's paired outcomes and in slice screens, beside the whole-case language rule; `PairedCase` adds `noise_reason` (`unstable_case` or `language_band`), and a flip of a stable case still counts. `GateVerdict` adds `slice_unstable_cases` (reported only, no new refusal). The noise view counts the classification and lists unstable and stable-fail cases; the paired view names each flip's noise reason and the band's classification; the slice view lists its unstable cases. Without a band every decision is unchanged. See the [evaluation-gym guide](docs/guides/evaluation-gym.md#the-noise-band-and-inconclusive).
