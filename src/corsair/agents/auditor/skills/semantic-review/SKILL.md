# Semantic review

1. Read only the supplied packet.
2. Check that the hypothesis matches the described production-source mechanism. Reject a materially false or unrelated claim; do not repair it into a different claim.
3. Check that the benign and adversarial payloads form a relevant contrast at the caller-controlled property.
4. Check that the pair invokes the real specified production callable with its fixed arguments, rather than a substitute or model-authored test.
5. Check that the stated observation is disclosure of the specified private fixture, not attacker-controlled echoing, success, or an arbitrary marker.
6. Use insufficient context when the packet cannot establish a required link. Reject when the packet establishes a contradiction or incoherent claim.
7. Do not create facts, execution evidence, coverage, private values, or negative clearance. Semantic acceptance is not a finding disposition.
