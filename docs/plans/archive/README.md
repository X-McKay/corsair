# Historical plans and evidence

Completed plans, handoffs, resolved issues and historical reviews move here from [active work](../README.md) once they are finished. They preserve decisions, attempted commands, failures, limitations and results at their recorded revisions; they are not current installation instructions. Start with [current work](../README.md) or the [documentation index](../../index.md) for current behavior.

| Directory | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| [`plans/`](plans/README.md) | Completed bounded plans with their recorded commands, results and unrun gates. |
| [`handoffs/`](handoffs/README.md) | Self-contained handoffs written at the end of substantial work. |
| [`issues/`](issues/README.md) | Resolved issues, including unsuccessful attempts. |
| [`reviews/`](reviews/README.md) | Historical review material superseded by current policy pages. |

Current security policy is [SECURITY.md](../../../SECURITY.md), the [security architecture](../../architecture/security.md) and the [packaged risk assessment](../../../src/corsair/policies/risk.md); archived reviews never override them. When a record moves here, update [current work](../README.md) and relative links in the same change.
